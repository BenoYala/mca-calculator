Why is an MCA so expensive?

Merchant Cash Advances (MCAs) are not traditional loans. They use a "factor rate" instead of an interest rate, which hides the true cost. A factor rate of 1.3 might look small, but when you calculate the APR based on the short repayment term, the real annual cost can exceed 60%, 80%, or even 100%. Our calculator helps you translate the factor rate into a standard APR, so you can make an informed decision before signing a contract.

How to use this calculator:

  • Funding Amount: The total amount of money you are borrowing.
  • Factor Rate: The multiplier applied to your funding amount (e.g., 1.25, 1.3, 1.4).
  • Term Length: How many months you have to repay the advance.
