Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit www.mca-apr.com and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you enter

The numbers you type into the calculators are processed in your browser. They are not sent to our servers and we do not store them. To be convenient, the site uses your browser's local storage to remember your last inputs, the offers you choose to save, and your checklist answers. This data stays on your device, we cannot see it, and you can remove it at any time by deleting saved items on the page or clearing your browser's site data.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically receive technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the pages you view. We use this kind of information to keep the site running, secure and improving.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. They may be used to remember preferences, understand how the site is used and to serve and measure advertising. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features of third-party services may not work if you block them.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law, such as for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site in aggregate. If we add them, we will keep this policy up to date.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion or opting out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising (for example under the California Consumer Privacy Act or the GDPR).
