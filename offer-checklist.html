How to use this checklist

Ask each question to the funder or broker and get the answers in writing. Tick the box once you have a clear answer, and use the note field to record it. When you are done, copy the summary to send to an advisor, a partner or your accountant.

The numbers behind your answers matter too. Run them through the APR calculator, then check the fit with your revenue in the payment calculator. For background, read our guide on fees and contract red flags.
