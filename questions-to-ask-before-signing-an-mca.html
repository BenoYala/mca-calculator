12 Questions to Ask Before Signing an MCA

Updated November 18, 2026 · 3 min read

The right questions can reveal the real cost of an offer before you commit. Use this checklist with the funder or broker, and ask for the answers in writing.

About the money

  1. What is the exact amount I will receive in my account? Subtract all fees from the headline amount.
  2. What is the total payback amount in dollars? Not just the factor rate.
  3. What is the estimated APR? If they cannot say, you can estimate it with our calculator.
  4. What fees apply, and when are they charged? Include origination, processing, broker and filing fees.

About repayment

  1. How much is each payment, and how often? Daily, weekly, or a percentage of sales.
  2. If payments are a percentage of sales, what happens in a slow month? Ask how and when reconciliation works.
  3. What happens if a payment bounces? Ask about fees and default definitions.
  4. Is there any discount for paying off early? If not, paying early may not reduce your cost.

About the contract

  1. Is there a personal guarantee or a lien on business assets?
  2. Who is the actual funder, and is a broker involved? Ask what the broker is paid.
  3. Are there restrictions on other financing? Ask about stacking clauses and cross-default terms.
  4. What are the default consequences and how are disputes handled?

What to do with the answers

  • Enter the amount, factor rate, term and fees into the APR calculator and compare with at least one other type of financing.
  • Check that the payment fits comfortably within your cash flow in your slowest months, not your best ones.
  • Compare the offer against alternatives in our MCA vs. business loan guide.
  • Consider free guidance from an SBA-supported Small Business Development Center or SCORE mentor before signing anything large.
Take your time. A legitimate offer should still be there after you have had a day to review it.
