About MCA APR

MCA APR is a free, independent website that helps business owners understand what a merchant cash advance (MCA) really costs. Factor rates are quoted as simple decimals, and it is hard to tell from a number like 1.3 whether an offer is expensive or reasonable. We built a calculator that turns the numbers on an offer into an estimated annual percentage rate (APR), plus guides that explain the terms in plain English.

What we do

  • Provide a free APR calculator that runs in your browser.
  • Publish educational guides on pricing, fees and contract terms.
  • Explain our methods, so you can check the math yourself.

What we are not

We are not a lender, broker, funder, law firm or financial advisor. We do not make or arrange financing, and we do not review contracts. The site is for general education and the figures it produces are estimates.

How we make our estimates

We divide the total payback (advance × factor rate) into equal daily or weekly payments, then solve for the periodic rate that makes those payments equal to the money you actually receive after fees. That rate is annualized into an APR. The method and its limits are described in our APR guide.

How the site is supported

The site may display advertising, including ads served by Google. Advertising does not influence our calculations or our guides. See our Privacy Policy for details about cookies and advertising.

Corrections and feedback

If you spot an error or have a suggestion, please get in touch. We update guides as rules and market practices change.
